# A7 – Creating a Linkage Mechanism

## Prompt
The objectives of this assignment were to design, print and document the process of making a working linkage mechanism. Said mechanism must be designed to achieve and fulfill a certain task or purpose. Other hardware that needed to be used to assemble the final mechanism can be purchased (screws, blots, rivets, etc.), but any parts outside of those in the final iteration must be of our own design and 3D print.

## Testing & Design
The main idea for the project was to make a stand that could be raised and lowered to multiple height settings to display something, the first idea being LEGO models. A simple mechanism was desired and something that could be set or disassembled easily if needed. Taking inspiration from the linkages that make up pliers and scissor lifts, the idea came to translate that into a lift that could hold my own LEGO models. Initial thoughts were sketched out as shown below:

fill

From the start the idea was always to have two links with a pin connecting them in the middle. Holes were written in for both ends of the linkages as well due the thought of easy expansion of the mechanism by adding more pairs of links to achieve more height. The snap end of the pin draws similarities to LEGO pin connectors. but the need to have a snap on both ends was not deemed necessary.

Realizing sturdy and sizable parts would be needed for the mechanism, design proceeded at a small scale first to test the fits and connections themselves to speed up development time and reduce waste prints. This centered around a central locking pin that would snap into holes in the middle of the links that would allow for free movement around the diameter of the pin but keep the links together. On top of that, the pin was to be removable as well from the snap fit.
