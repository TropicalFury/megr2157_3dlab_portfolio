# L03 – Design & Print Something Small

## Prompt
The L03 design instructions called for us to design a small object. The goal of the lab was to learn about the different uses and iterations of infills and implement then in a print to gain more experience with 3D printing in general. This process was broken down into a few steps that are described as follows:

1. Design the small object using a parametric CAD system.
2. Research three infills different from the ones presented in class and describe the uses for each.
3. Use PrusaSlicer to change the default infill percentage, pattern, and wall thickness settings.
4. Print the design using one of the Prusa Core One 3D printers available in the UNCC print farm.
5. Provide answers to how the percentage infill and infill patterns affect mechanical properties and why different wall thicknesses may be used.

Along with these steps, the object itself had some stipulations:

- No more than 0.5 inch tall
- No overhangs (a deliberate simplification for this lab, supports are covered in a later lab)
- Large enough to use infill
- Modify wall thickness
- Print in PLA or PETG
- No more than 1.5 inches by 1.5 inches
- Print time < 1.5 hours; confirm this using PrusaSlicer's estimated print time before sending to print
- The print farm has limited printer capacity. Students will work in teams of 2 to 4 and print their artifacts together on the same build platform.
- Remember, enjoy and learn from the process.

## Design
With all the above stated, I moved onto the design process starting with the choice of my object. At moment of object choice I had no ideas for something that could be used or serve a particular function, so I looked to things one could play with or symbols that had meaning to me. Having recently seen one as a tattoo during the summer and being a longtime fan of the video game series HALO, I decided to make my object the NOBLE team logo as first depicted in the game HALO Reach. In said game players control one of the members of the previously mentioned NOBLE team, a squad of human super-soldiers who participate in the defense of the planet Reach against a massive invasion from the alien conglomerate known as The Covenant.

With a design in mind, I set to work making it in my CAD software of choice, SOLIDWORKS. The logo has a lot of negative space so in order to have the object meet the design stipulations of being large enough to use infill, I decided to make the negative space be inlaid and have the actual parts of the logo pop out. This led to a design using two extrusions, one to set the negative space as a base plate on the bottom and then extruding the logo's features out on top of that. With a simple design such as what was chosen, overhangs were not a worry, just getting the angles and lengths of the symbol's features as correct as I could.
