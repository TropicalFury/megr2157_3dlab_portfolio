## Additive Manufacturing Research
**Find one design rule specific to additive manufacturing:**
2022 ASME Y14.46 Additive Manufacturing Standard 4.3.1: “If a build direction is required, it shall be identified using the model’s coordinate system and unit vector and shall be consistent with X, Y, Z defined in ISO/ASTM 52921. The unit vector shall be labeled “B DIR” for build direction.”

This standard states how build direction for additive manufacturing must be specified in drawings if it is necessary. It must also be in line with the already established coordinate system in the drawing for consistency and proper manufacture. This is important because something small like this could be easy to overlook and assume is the same for all parts. It also makes sense to carry over the required established coordinate standard from 3.1 and 3.2 over to build direction and other necessary directions.
